SCANNING THE LAN
This pod runs on the cluster network, so traffic to {{ .Values.gateway.hostname }}'s
subnet is SNAT'd to the node's address. TCP, UDP, ICMP and raw/SYN probes all work.
ARP ping does not — the pod is not on the LAN's L2 segment. Greenbone ORs its alive
tests, so hosts answering ICMP or any TCP port are still discovered; a host that
blocks ICMP *and* has no open TCP port will be missed. If you hit that, set the
target's Alive Test to "Consider Alive" in the UI.
